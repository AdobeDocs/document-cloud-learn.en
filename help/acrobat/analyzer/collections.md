# Use Collections

Analyzer in Acrobat Studio helps you turn large sets of documents into structured, cited insights. Collections make that process easier by grouping related files so you can apply the right attributes, filter results, and review only the information that matters. In this video, learn how to create manual and linked Collections, apply attributes, and keep documents organized as your content grows.
