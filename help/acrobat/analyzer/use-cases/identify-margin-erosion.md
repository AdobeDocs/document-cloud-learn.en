# Subcontract management for identification of eroding margin source detection

Project margin isn't lost at the prime-contract level. It's lost one subcontract at a time — in change orders that grew before anyone priced the recovery, in RFIs that aged quietly into delay claims, and in contract protections that never made it into the sub's paper. By the time it shows up in a report, the money is already gone. Learn how to identify hidden margin erosion risks in subcontract agreements by uncovering missed change orders, aging RFIs, and contract gaps before they impact project profitability.
